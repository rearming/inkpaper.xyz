diffuza

Founding full-stack engineer · Mar 2026 — now · diffuza.com

Real-time AI visuals for clubs, festivals and installations. The image follows the music and is controlled from a browser studio. I work on all of it: the renderer, the studio, cloud GPU rigs and the MCP server. It has been used at events in BangkokUnfest, BerlinBKK, Avve, Dual, Soho House Bangkok; nights by Sacristy, ALTER EGO and COUNTERCULTURAL. since spring 2026.

  • RendererSDXL-Turbo stream diffusion on TensorRT, written to replace the third-party engine the project started with. 30 fps at 512×896 on one RTX 5080, with latent feedback, IP-Adapter, ControlNet and depth.
  • OptimisationFull-VAE frame from 129 to 55 ms: static-shape TensorRT engines, so GroupNorm fusion works; CUDA graphs; a small refiner I trained so the fast decoder gets close to full-VAE quality.
  • MCP server16 tools. Claude, ChatGPT or Codex can read and change pipelines, inputs, clock, renderer and DMX lights, and take a screenshot of the render machine.
  • StudioMulti-user browser studio, cloud GPU rigs on Runpod with credits and billing, a desktop app for the venue PC, 30 input modules from beat detection to hand tracking.
